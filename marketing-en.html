    Digital Marketing

    From strategy and content, to data, analytics, and evidence-based decision-making.

    SEO Analytics Data-Driven Marketing Strategy

    Digital Marketing στον ψηφιακό κόσμο

    Modern digital marketing is not limited to promoting a product or service. It is based on understanding the audience, measuring user behavior, and leveraging data to improve strategy.

    SEO

    Optimizing a website's organic presence and creating content that meets the user's needs and search intent.

    Social Media

    Content strategy, community building, engagement, and performance evaluation of social media activities.

    Content Marketing

    Creation of useful and targeted content that enhances brand awareness and the relationship with the audience.

    Digital Advertising

    Planning and evaluation of digital advertising campaigns aimed at the effective use of the budget.

    Email Marketing

    Targeted communication, segmentation, automation, and evaluation of recipient behavior.

    Marketing Analytics

    Transforming marketing data into insights that can support strategic and operational decisions.

    Marketing + Data

    The true value of digital marketing lies in connecting strategy with measurement and data analysis.

    Data

    Data collection from websites, campaigns, social media, search engines, and digital channels.

    Analytics

    Analysis of user behavior, conversion funnels, campaigns, and key performance indicators.

    Insights

    Transforming analyses into actionable insights and evidence-based marketing decisions.
